GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can use ZeroLag without creating an account.

Are my videos uploaded?

No. Video processing happens locally in your browser. The external libraries are loaded from their CDN addresses.

Why can a large GIF take time?

GIF files contain many individual image frames. Larger dimensions and higher FPS require more processing.

Which video formats work?

Supported formats depend on your browser. MP4 and WebM are commonly supported.
