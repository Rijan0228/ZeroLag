GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Does ZeroLag upload my videos?

No. The application uses browser-based video processing. Your selected video is processed locally and is not uploaded to a ZeroLag backend.

Why is GIF generation sometimes slow?

GIF files can contain many individual frames. Higher resolutions, longer durations and higher frame rates require more processing and memory.

Why can't I convert a very long video?

ZeroLag currently limits GIF duration to 30 seconds and frame extraction to 300 frames to reduce excessive browser resource use.

Which video formats are supported?

Support depends on your browser. MP4 and WebM are commonly supported, while other formats depend on the codecs available on your device.

Can I use ZeroLag on mobile?

Yes. The interface is responsive, but processing large videos may be slower on phones with limited memory.
